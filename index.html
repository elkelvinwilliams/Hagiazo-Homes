Landlord & Investor Partnership Models

Transparent, legally secured leasing frameworks tailored to the income certainty you need and the involvement you want.

Guaranteed Rent RouteZero Voids

Guaranteed Rent Landlord

For owners who prefer fixed predictability. We lease your property on a company let and pay an agreed rent every month for the full term, occupied or not.

  • Fixed Rent: Agreed monthly figure paid on the 1st for a 3–7 year term
  • Void Protection: Rent continues through vacancies and tenant changeovers
  • Upkeep Covered: Routine maintenance and compliance handled in-house
  • Minimum Term: 36-month company lease with pre-agreed rent reviews
Income Partner RouteCo-Investment · Income Share

Portfolio Income Partner

For investors seeking recurring income. Fund the set-up of leased homes — deposits, furnishing, refurbishment — and share in the net rental surplus across the portfolio.

  • Income Split: 60/40 net rental surplus in your favour, distributed quarterly
  • Term Length: 24–36 month partnership term with renewal option
  • Security: Registered charge or SPV shareholding options
  • Target Return: 12%–15% p.a. depending on portfolio mix

Essential clarity on our lease agreements, rent guarantees, void handling, and reporting protocols.

Your rent is a contractual obligation under a solicitor-drafted company lease between you and Hagiazo Homes Ltd. The rent is fixed for the term and payable on the 1st of every month whether or not the property is occupied, and the agreement sets out the condition in which the home will be returned to you.

Key Governance Safeguards
  • Company lease drafted by independent UK SRA-regulated solicitors.
  • Rent paid by standing order from the operating company, never dependent on a tenant paying first.
  • Photographic schedule of condition signed at the start and checked at every quarterly inspection.

Guaranteed Rent Landlords receive their agreed monthly rent on the 1st of each month from the lease start date, with a statement emailed the same day. Portfolio Income Partners receive 60% of the net rental surplus across the homes they have funded, distributed quarterly with a full income and cost schedule. Monthly distributions are available for commitments of £100,000 or greater.

Key Governance Safeguards
  • Owner rent is paid before any Hagiazo surplus is taken.
  • Income Partner distributions reconcile to bank statements and the quarterly schedule.
  • Flexible rollover: re-deploy surplus into the next lease batch to compound income.

Our standard company lease runs for 36 months, with most owners choosing five or seven years for maximum certainty. We lease houses, flats, licensed HMOs and whole blocks in our regional hubs, provided the property can be brought to a compliant letting standard within our set-up budget. Rent reviews are agreed at signing, typically annually in line with CPI or a fixed step.

Key Governance Safeguards
  • Minimum 36-month term; 60 and 84-month options available.
  • Break clauses only where both parties agree them at signing.
  • Set-up works are funded by Hagiazo, not deducted from the owner's rent.

Nothing changes for you. Arrears, possession proceedings and void periods are Hagiazo's risk under the lease. Your rent continues on the 1st of every month. Our 98.2% historical occupancy rests on multiple occupier channels — professional lets, contractor stays and Registered Provider referrals — so a vacancy is re-let rather than absorbed.

Key Governance Safeguards
  • Owner rent is unaffected by arrears or voids.

  • Rent guarantee covenant backed by the operating company and founder capital.
